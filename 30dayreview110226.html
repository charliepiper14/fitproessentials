Root Cause Analysis: Why Performance Declined

Four things went wrong at once: (1) Facebook account lockout during Jan 22-26 prevented budget management and optimization during a critical period; (2) Multiple simultaneous changes on Jan 27 relaunch (new ad sets, new audiences, new creative, budget changes) all triggered learning phase resets; (3) Warm audience saturation accelerated by the 5-day gap where spend continued without optimization; (4) We were testing a lot of creative but stacking too many changes at once, so we could not tell what was actually working and what was not. The original Opened structure that delivered $8-10 CPAs was not preserved through the transition.

What Changed

The SamCart to GoHighLevel checkout migration resolved the main tracking issue. Sugar Detox runs entirely through GHL with accurate pixel firing. The 6WK campaign still shows a slight gap (56 Meta vs 54 confirmed), with Meta slightly over-reporting likely due to view-through attribution counting some warm audience exposures. What this means: the pre-takeover period's 8.29x ROAS was partially inflated by unattributed sales that may not all have been directly caused by ads. However, even adjusting for this, the pre-takeover numbers were still much stronger than what we are seeing now.

Strategic Implication

With attribution now reliable, we can trust Meta's optimization signals going forward. This means the algorithm can properly learn from actual conversions rather than partially blind data. Cleaner data means better optimization over time. Now that tracking is sorted, the focus shifts entirely to getting CPA down through better creative and smarter campaign structure.

Bottom Line

Month 1 was a rough start.
Here is exactly how we fix it.

The numbers do not lie. We are spending more and getting less than the pre-takeover period. CPA went from $6.32 to $44.33, and the account is running at a loss. That has to change fast. But the data also shows us exactly where the money should be going: static creative with coach-specific callouts, price anchoring copy, and a lower-friction entry point through Sugar Detox. The funnel backend is actually stronger (OB +44%, upsells +21%, new customers +133%), and attribution is now accurate. The problem is top-of-funnel efficiency, and the fix is straightforward: stop wasting budget on video creative that does not convert, double down on the statics and copy angles that do, and test one thing at a time instead of changing everything at once.
